# Symptoms

The performance symptoms the bench covers. Generated by `bench/make_lists.py` from `bench/symptoms.json` and `bench/scenarios.json`.

There are 48 symptoms in 7 groups. Each has a written criterion for **present**. Each is made present by at least one fault of the nine bench programs (`<program> --fault <fault>`). On every other trace of that program it is absent, unless the program's answer key marks it *don't care*.

| Group | Symptoms |
|---|---|
| Timing | 7 |
| Waiting | 12 |
| CPU | 11 |
| System calls and I/O | 7 |
| Lifecycle | 4 |
| Pipelines | 4 |
| Unexpected behaviour | 3 |
| **All** | 48 |

Events a symptom needs, beyond those every trace has:

- `base`: sched_*, timer/IRQ, x86 IRQ vectors, all syscalls, statedump (always recorded)
- `net`: net_*, netif_* (packets sent and received)
- `block`: block_* (disk requests issued and completed)
- `signal`: signal_generate, signal_deliver
- `mem`: kmem_* and page-fault events (no probe for them on this machine's kernel)

## Timing (7)

| Symptom | Present when | How users ask | Shown by | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **One-off delay or missed deadline** (`one_off_delay`) | a single iteration (or a few in one stretch) of an otherwise regular activity takes at least 5x its usual time | “it got delayed once”; “one request took 15 ms” | `ticker --fault one_off_delay` | Q36 (`ticker`) (quantitative, single-hub) |
| **Jitter** (`jitter`) | a periodic activity's lateness or duration varies continuously, by a quarter of its period or more, throughout the run | “the timing varies a lot”; “it's not steady” | `ticker --fault jitter` | Q37 (`ticker`) (quantitative, single-hub) |
| **Many slow outliers (tail latency)** (`tail_latency`) | a small share (about 1-5%) of requests or iterations are many times slower than the median, spread over the whole run | “some requests are slow”; “the p99 is bad” | `kvstore --fault tail_latency` | Q44 (`kvstore`) (quantitative, multi-hub) |
| **Gradual slowdown** (`gradual_slowdown`) | iterations get steadily slower from the start of the run to the end, to at least twice their initial length | “it gets slower over time” | `ticker --fault gradual_slowdown` | Q38 (`ticker`) (quantitative, single-hub) |
| **Sudden change** (`sudden_change`) | iterations are fast up to one point in the run and at least twice as slow from then on (a step) | “it was fine, then got slow around 40 s” | `ticker --fault sudden_change` | Q39 (`ticker`) (quantitative, single-hub) |
| **Slow start-up** (`slow_startup`) | the program takes seconds from its start to its first unit of work | “it takes long to start doing work” | `jobrunner --fault slow_startup` | Q1 (`jobrunner`) (comprehension, single-hub) |
| **Slow shutdown** (`slow_shutdown`) | the program takes seconds from its last unit of work to its exit | “it takes forever to exit” | `jobrunner --fault slow_shutdown` | Q14 (`jobrunner`) (root cause, multi-hub) |

## Waiting (12)

| Symptom | Present when | How users ask | Shown by | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Long blocked waits** (`long_blocked_waits`) | a thread that has work to do is blocked for a quarter or more of its time, or for seconds at a time (not counting idle waits for new work or its own timed sleeps) | “it waits a long time”; “it's blocked a lot” | `batch_server --fault holder_sleeps`<br>`batch_server --fault deadlock`<br>`batch_server --fault hang`<br>`batch_server --fault priority_inversion`<br>`kvstore --fault network_wait` | Q26 (`batch_server`), Q27 (`kvstore`) (dependency, single-hub) |
| **Slowest thread or bottleneck** (`bottleneck_thread`) | one thread (or one stage) holds up the whole program, which would run at least 25% faster without its delay | “why is the program slow?”; “what's the bottleneck?” | `batch_server --fault holder_sleeps`<br>`batch_server --fault priority_inversion`<br>`crunch --fault load_imbalance`<br>`crunch --fault barrier_straggler`<br>`crunch --fault cpu_starvation`<br>`pipeline --fault slow_stage`<br>`kvstore --fault slow_backend` | Q15 (`batch_server`), Q16 (`crunch`), Q17 (`pipeline`), Q18 (`kvstore`) (root cause, multi-hub) |
| **Hang or freeze** (`hang`) | the program stops making progress for good: its threads stay blocked until the end of the trace | “it's stuck”; “it stopped responding” | `batch_server --fault deadlock`<br>`batch_server --fault hang` | Q19 (`batch_server`) (root cause, multi-hub) |
| **Deadlock** (`deadlock`) | two or more threads each hold a lock the other needs, and stay blocked for good | “two things are waiting on each other” | `batch_server --fault deadlock` | Q30 (`batch_server`) (dependency, multi-hub) |
| **Lock contention** (`lock_contention`) | threads block on the same lock many times per second because another thread holds it | “threads fight over a lock” | `batch_server --fault contention`<br>`batch_server --fault convoy` | Q31 (`batch_server`) (dependency, multi-hub) |
| **Lock holder sleeping or doing I/O while holding it** (`lock_holder_blocking`) | a lock is held across a sleep, I/O or a preemption, and other threads wait on it meanwhile | “something holds the lock too long” | `batch_server --fault holder_sleeps` | Q54 (`batch_server`) (remediation, multi-hub) |
| **Convoy or serialization** (`convoy`) | threads that could run in parallel run one at a time because of a shared lock | “the threads run one after another instead of in parallel” | `batch_server --fault convoy` | Q32 (`batch_server`) (dependency, multi-hub) |
| **Priority inversion** (`priority_inversion`) | a high-priority thread waits on a lock held by a lower-priority thread, which is itself kept off the CPU by a medium-priority thread | “the high-priority task waits on a low-priority one” | `batch_server --fault priority_inversion` | Q33 (`batch_server`) (dependency, multi-hub) |
| **Waiting on another process** (`waiting_on_process`) | threads spend a quarter or more of their time blocked waiting for replies from another process | “it's waiting on the database or the other service” | `kvstore --fault slow_backend` | Q34 (`kvstore`) (dependency, multi-hub) |
| **Waiting on network** (`waiting_on_network`) | a thread spends a quarter or more of its time blocked on network communication with another machine | “it's slow talking to the server” | `kvstore --fault network_wait` | Q28 (`kvstore`) (dependency, single-hub) |
| **Waiting on disk** (`waiting_on_disk`) | a thread spends a quarter or more of its time blocked on storage I/O (writes, syncs, reads) | “saving is slow”; “fsync takes long” | `filestore --fault disk_wait` | Q29 (`filestore`) (dependency, single-hub) |
| **Oversleeping** (`oversleeping`) | most sleeps end 1 ms or more later than requested while the CPU is free (late timer expiry, not CPU contention) | “the timer fires late”; “it sleeps longer than asked” | `ticker --fault oversleeping` | Q12 (`ticker`) (root cause, single-hub) |

## CPU (11)

| Symptom | Present when | How users ask | Shown by | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CPU hog or busiest thread** (`cpu_hog`) | one thread keeps a CPU close to 100% busy for most of the run | “what's using all the CPU?”; “which thread is busiest?” | `crunch --fault cpu_hog`<br>`crunch --fault busy_wait` | Q45 (`crunch`) (quantitative, multi-hub) |
| **Slow to get scheduled** (`scheduling_delay`) | a runnable thread is kept waiting to run for 1 ms or more at least 50 times in the trace, or for 10 ms or more at least once | “it's ready but has to wait for the CPU”; “scheduling delay” | `ticker --fault one_off_delay`<br>`ticker --fault jitter`<br>`ticker --fault rt_preempted`<br>`crunch --fault barrier_straggler`<br>`crunch --fault cpu_starvation`<br>`crunch --fault oversubscription`<br>`reporter --fault neighbour` | Q40 (`ticker`), Q41 (`crunch`), Q42 (`reporter`) (quantitative, single-hub) |
| **Busy-waiting or spinning** (`busy_wait`)<br>*some traces cannot show it* | a thread uses a full CPU while waiting for something, making no progress | “high CPU but nothing gets done” | `crunch --fault busy_wait` | Q2 (`crunch`) (comprehension, single-hub) |
| **CPU starvation** (`cpu_starvation`) | a runnable thread gets (almost) no CPU time for a long stretch (seconds) | “it's ready but never gets to run” | `crunch --fault cpu_starvation` | Q20 (`crunch`) (root cause, multi-hub) |
| **Noisy neighbour** (`noisy_neighbour`) | another process competes for the program's CPUs and slows its work down by 25% or more | “another program is slowing mine down” | `reporter --fault neighbour` | Q21 (`reporter`) (root cause, multi-hub) |
| **Load imbalance** (`load_imbalance`) | work is unevenly divided: some threads of a group get at least twice as much of it as others | “one worker does all the work”; “uneven load” | `crunch --fault load_imbalance`<br>`pipeline --fault idle_workers` | Q46 (`crunch`), Q47 (`pipeline`) (quantitative, multi-hub) |
| **Straggler at a barrier** (`barrier_straggler`) | threads that meet at a barrier keep waiting for the same late thread | “everyone waits for one slow thread” | `crunch --fault barrier_straggler`<br>`crunch --fault cpu_starvation` | Q22 (`crunch`) (root cause, multi-hub) |
| **Oversubscription** (`oversubscription`) | at least twice as many runnable threads as CPUs, so threads queue for the CPU | “too many threads for the cores” | `crunch --fault oversubscription` | Q55 (`crunch`) (remediation, multi-hub) |
| **Excessive migrations** (`excessive_migrations`) | threads move between CPUs very often (hundreds of times per second) | “it keeps jumping between CPUs” | `crunch --fault migrations` | Q50 (`crunch`) (remediation, single-hub) |
| **Interrupt storm** (`interrupt_storm`) | interrupt handling (hardware interrupt handlers) takes a large share of one CPU, 10% or more for seconds at a time, where a CPU normally spends about 1% in them | “interrupts are eating the CPU” | `reporter --fault irq_storm` | Q23 (`reporter`) (root cause, multi-hub) |
| **Real-time thread preempted** (`rt_preempted`) | a real-time (SCHED_FIFO/RR) thread is repeatedly preempted by a higher-priority one | “my real-time thread gets interrupted” | `ticker --fault rt_preempted` | Q24 (`ticker`) (root cause, multi-hub) |

## System calls and I/O (7)

| Symptom | Present when | How users ask | Shown by | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Slow system calls or I/O** (`slow_syscalls`) | a thread spends 10% or more of its time waiting inside system calls (not counting sleeps or waits for events), some of which take milliseconds | “which calls are slow?”; “file access is slow” | `filestore --fault disk_wait` | Q43 (`filestore`) (quantitative, single-hub) |
| **Too many small calls** (`many_small_calls`) | the program makes tens of thousands of tiny system calls per second where a few large ones would do | “it makes thousands of tiny reads” | `filestore --fault small_calls` | Q51 (`filestore`) (remediation, single-hub) |
| **Polling with no work** (`idle_polling`) | a thread repeatedly checks for work or data (poll, select, read with no wait) that is never there | “it keeps checking for nothing” | `filestore --fault polling` | Q52 (`filestore`) (remediation, single-hub) |
| **Failing calls in a retry loop** (`failing_retry_loop`) | the same system call fails over and over (an error return each time) | “it keeps retrying”; “lots of errors” | `filestore --fault retry_loop` | Q3 (`filestore`) (comprehension, single-hub) |
| **File churn** (`file_churn`) | files are created, opened, closed or deleted at a high rate (hundreds per second) | “it opens and closes files constantly” | `filestore --fault file_churn` | Q4 (`filestore`) (comprehension, single-hub) |
| **Memory pressure or page faults** (`memory_pressure`)<br>*needs `mem` events; some traces cannot show it* | the program spends a quarter or more of its time in page faults or memory reclaim | “it's swapping”; “memory is slow” | `cachesvc --fault memory_pressure` | Q13 (`cachesvc`) (root cause, single-hub) |
| **Allocation churn** (`allocation_churn`) | memory is mapped and unmapped (mmap/munmap, brk) hundreds of times per second or more | “it keeps allocating and freeing memory” | `cachesvc --fault allocation_churn` | Q53 (`cachesvc`) (remediation, single-hub) |

## Lifecycle (4)

| Symptom | Present when | How users ask | Shown by | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Crash or abnormal exit** (`crash`)<br>*needs `signal` events; some traces cannot show it* | a process of the program dies from a signal (segfault, abort, kill) rather than exiting | “it crashed”; “it died”; “it got killed” | `jobrunner --fault crash` | Q5 (`jobrunner`) (comprehension, single-hub) |
| **Fork or exec storm** (`fork_exec_storm`) | the program starts short-lived processes at a high rate (tens per second or more) | “it spawns tons of short processes” | `jobrunner --fault fork_storm` | Q9 (`jobrunner`) (comprehension, multi-hub) |
| **Thread churn** (`thread_churn`) | the program creates and ends threads at a high rate (tens per second or more) | “it keeps creating threads” | `jobrunner --fault thread_churn` | Q10 (`jobrunner`) (comprehension, multi-hub) |
| **Never exits** (`never_exits`) | the program has finished its work but its process stays alive until the end of the trace | “it doesn't quit” | `jobrunner --fault never_exits` | Q6 (`jobrunner`) (comprehension, single-hub) |

## Pipelines (4)

| Symptom | Present when | How users ask | Shown by | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Slowest pipeline stage** (`slowest_stage`) | one stage of a pipeline is too slow for its input, and the stages around it wait for it | “which stage is the bottleneck?” | `pipeline --fault slow_stage` | Q25 (`pipeline`) (root cause, multi-hub) |
| **Producer and consumer out of step** (`producer_consumer_mismatch`) | a queue between two threads keeps swinging between full and empty | “the queue keeps filling or emptying” | `pipeline --fault bursty` | Q35 (`pipeline`) (dependency, multi-hub) |
| **Thundering herd** (`thundering_herd`) | each event wakes many waiting threads, and all but one go straight back to sleep | “many threads wake up but only one does anything” | `pipeline --fault herd` | Q56 (`pipeline`) (remediation, multi-hub) |
| **Idle workers** (`idle_workers`) | most workers of a pool stay idle while one or a few do all the work | “the workers are idle while one works” | `pipeline --fault idle_workers` | Q11 (`pipeline`) (comprehension, multi-hub) |

## Unexpected behaviour (3)

| Symptom | Present when | How users ask | Shown by | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Unexpected activity** (`unexpected_activity`) | the program does something unrelated to its job (here: reads files that have nothing to do with it) | “what is this process doing?”; “why is it touching that file?” | `filestore --fault unexpected_scan` | Q7 (`filestore`) (comprehension, single-hub) |
| **Idle thread** (`idle_thread`) | a thread exists for the whole run but never does any work | “this thread does nothing” | `pipeline --fault idle_thread` | Q8 (`pipeline`) (comprehension, single-hub) |
| **Thread differences** (`thread_differences`) | one thread of a group of similar threads behaves differently in a way the rest do not share (e.g. at least twice their CPU time, blocking, or system calls of a kind they make none of) | “why does worker-2 behave differently from the others?” | `batch_server --fault holder_sleeps`<br>`batch_server --fault priority_inversion`<br>`crunch --fault load_imbalance`<br>`crunch --fault odd_worker` | Q48 (`batch_server`), Q49 (`crunch`) (quantitative, multi-hub) |
